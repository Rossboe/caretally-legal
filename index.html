Deleting your CareTally data

CareTally has no accounts — the app never asks you to sign up, and we do not hold your business records. Deleting your data works like this:

Data stored on your device

Your clients, shifts, notes, invoices and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling CareTally permanently deletes all of it. There is no copy on our servers and nothing further to request. (If you exported backup files, those are yours — delete them from wherever you saved them.)

Your cloud backup

If you turned on cloud backup (a Pro feature, off by default), a copy of your data is stored under the email address you signed in with. To delete it, either sign out and use the delete option in the app, or email rosscodaking@gmail.com from that address. We will delete the backup and the account within 30 days and confirm by reply. If you never turned cloud backup on, we hold nothing.

Feedback you sent us

If you used the in-app Send feedback form, the message (and email address, if you included one) is stored with our database provider. To have it deleted, email rosscodaking@gmail.com with the subject "CareTally data deletion request" and, if possible, the approximate date you sent the feedback. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.
