CareTally Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

CareTally is a shift-logging and invoicing app for independent NDIS support workers, made in Australia. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your business data stays on your device unless you choose to turn on cloud backup.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter to run your business is stored locally on your phone:

  • Your business details (name, ABN, contact and bank details)
  • Client details (names, NDIS numbers, plan management and contact information)
  • Shifts, shift notes, travel and invoices
  • Your app PIN and settings

Unless you turn on cloud backup, none of this is transmitted to us or anyone else. Invoices you generate are shared only when and where you choose to send them, using your device's own share function. Backup files you export are files you control — store them somewhere safe, because we have no copy and cannot recover them for you.

Cloud backup (optional, for Pro subscribers)

CareTally Pro includes an optional cloud backup, so that a lost, broken or replaced phone does not cost you your records. It is off unless you turn it on. If you do turn it on:

  • You sign in with your email address, so your backup can be matched to you on a replacement phone. We email you a one-time code; there is no password.
  • A copy of your CareTally data — including client names, NDIS numbers, shift notes and invoices — is stored on servers operated by our database provider, Supabase, and is sent over an encrypted connection.
  • Only your signed-in account can read your backup. This is enforced at the database level, not just in the app.
  • Your backup is replaced each time it updates. We do not keep a history of previous versions.
  • You can turn cloud backup off, sign out, or ask us to delete your backup and your account at any time by emailing us.

If you never turn cloud backup on, your data never leaves your device.

Data you choose to send us

If you use the in-app Send feedback form, we receive the message you write, the category you pick, your app version and platform, and — only if you enter it — an email address for replies. Feedback is stored securely with our database provider (Supabase) and used solely to improve CareTally. To have your feedback deleted, email us at the address below.

What we don't do

  • No account is required — the app works without telling us who you are, and you only sign in if you choose to use cloud backup
  • No advertising and no sale of data
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera or photos

Payments

CareTally Pro subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Data deletion

Deleting the app deletes all locally stored data. For feedback you have submitted, email us and we will delete it.

Changes
